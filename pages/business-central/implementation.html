Our Implementation Process

We follow a proven methodology to ensure successful Business Central implementation

1

Discovery & Analysis

We begin by understanding your business processes, requirements, and goals. This phase includes detailed discussions with stakeholders, process mapping, and gap analysis.

2

Solution Design

Based on our analysis, we design a tailored Business Central solution that addresses your specific needs. This includes configuration planning, customization requirements, and integration mapping.

3

Development & Configuration

Our team configures Business Central according to the solution design, develops any required customizations, and sets up integrations with other systems.

4

Data Migration

We carefully migrate your data from existing systems to Business Central, ensuring data integrity and completeness throughout the process.

5

Testing & Validation

Comprehensive testing is conducted to ensure all components work correctly, including system testing, integration testing, and user acceptance testing.

6

Training

We provide thorough training to your team, ensuring they understand how to use Business Central effectively for their specific roles.

7

Go-Live & Support

We manage the go-live process and provide post-implementation support to ensure a smooth transition and address any issues that arise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Business Central implementation

How long does a typical Business Central implementation take?

The duration of a Business Central implementation varies depending on the complexity of your business processes, the scope of customizations, and the size of your organization. A basic implementation can take 2-3 months, while more complex implementations may take 4-6 months or longer. During our initial consultation, we'll provide a more accurate timeline based on your specific requirements.

Can we continue using our business during implementation?

Yes, our implementation methodology is designed to minimize disruption to your daily operations. We work closely with your team to plan the implementation in phases, ensuring that critical business functions remain operational throughout the process. The final cutover to the new system is carefully planned to occur during a period of minimal business activity.

How much does Business Central implementation cost?

The cost of Business Central implementation depends on various factors, including the size of your organization, the complexity of your business processes, the extent of customizations required, and the number of users. We offer flexible pricing options and can provide a detailed quote after understanding your specific requirements during our initial consultation.

Can you migrate data from our existing systems?

Yes, data migration is a key part of our implementation process. We have experience migrating data from various systems, including legacy ERP systems, accounting software, spreadsheets, and databases. Our team will develop a comprehensive data migration strategy to ensure all your critical business data is accurately transferred to Business Central.

What training do you provide during implementation?

We provide comprehensive training tailored to different user roles within your organization. This includes system administrator training, power user training, and end-user training. We offer both group and one-on-one training sessions, as well as documentation and training materials that your team can reference after implementation. Our goal is to ensure your team is confident and proficient in using Business Central for their daily tasks.
