Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Business Central licensing

What's the difference between Essentials and Premium licenses?

The main difference between Essentials and Premium licenses is the functionality they provide. Essentials includes core financial and business management functionality, suitable for most small to medium-sized businesses. Premium includes everything in Essentials plus advanced features for manufacturing and service management, such as production orders, service order management, and advanced warehouse management. If your business involves manufacturing or complex service management, you may need Premium licenses. Otherwise, Essentials is typically sufficient for most businesses.

Can we mix different license types?

Yes, you can mix different license types within your Business Central environment. For example, you can have some users with Essentials licenses, some with Premium licenses, and others with Team Member licenses. This allows you to optimize your licensing costs by providing each user with the appropriate level of access based on their role. We can help you determine the optimal mix of license types for your specific business needs and user roles.

What happens to our licenses if we move from on-premises to cloud?

When moving from on-premises to cloud deployment, your licensing model will change from perpetual licensing to subscription-based licensing. Microsoft offers transition paths for existing on-premises customers, which may include license transition benefits or discounts. The specific details depend on your current licensing agreement and the timing of your migration. We can help you navigate this transition, ensuring you understand the implications for your licensing costs and helping you optimize your new subscription-based licensing configuration.

How are add-ons and extensions licensed?

Add-ons and extensions for Business Central typically have their own licensing models, which vary depending on the provider. Some add-ons are licensed per user, similar to Business Central itself, while others may be licensed per company, per server, or based on usage metrics. Partner solutions like Jet Reports, Tasklet Factory, Continia, and Dime Scheduler each have their own licensing models. We can help you understand the licensing requirements for any add-ons or extensions you're considering, ensuring you have a complete picture of your total licensing costs.

How often do licensing terms change?

Microsoft typically reviews and updates its licensing terms annually, though significant changes are usually announced well in advance. These changes may include pricing adjustments, changes to license types, or modifications to included functionality. As part of our licensing services, we stay up-to-date with Microsoft's licensing changes and proactively inform our clients about any changes that may affect them. We also provide guidance on how to adapt to these changes in the most cost-effective way.
