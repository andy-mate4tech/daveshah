Our Recovery Process

A systematic approach to resolving Business Central issues

1

Initial Assessment

We begin with a comprehensive assessment of your Business Central system to identify the nature and extent of the issues. This includes gathering information about system behavior, error messages, and recent changes.

2

Diagnostic Analysis

Our experts conduct a detailed diagnostic analysis to determine the root causes of the issues. This involves examining system logs, database integrity, customizations, and integration points.

3

Recovery Planning

Based on the diagnostic results, we develop a comprehensive recovery plan tailored to your specific situation. This plan outlines the steps, timeline, and resources required for successful recovery.

4

Implementation

We implement the recovery plan, applying the necessary fixes, repairs, and optimizations to restore your Business Central system. This may include data recovery, code fixes, configuration changes, or system restoration.

5

Testing & Validation

We thoroughly test the recovered system to ensure all issues have been resolved and the system is functioning correctly. This includes functional testing, performance testing, and data validation.

6

Preventive Recommendations

We provide detailed recommendations to prevent similar issues in the future, including best practices, system monitoring, and maintenance procedures.

7

Knowledge Transfer

We share our findings and solutions with your team, ensuring they understand the issues that occurred and how to prevent them in the future.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our recovery services

How quickly can you respond to a critical system issue?

For critical system issues that significantly impact your business operations, we provide emergency response services with initial response times typically within 2-4 hours. Our team will begin remote diagnosis immediately and, if necessary, can arrange for on-site support depending on your location. We prioritize critical issues to minimize business disruption and work continuously until the issue is resolved.

Can you recover lost or corrupted data in Business Central?

Yes, we have extensive experience recovering lost or corrupted data in Business Central environments. Our data recovery processes can restore data from backups, repair corrupted database tables, and reconstruct data relationships. The success rate depends on several factors, including the nature of the corruption, the availability of backups, and the time elapsed since the issue occurred. In most cases, we can recover the majority of critical business data.

How do you ensure the issue won't happen again?

As part of our recovery process, we conduct a thorough root cause analysis to identify the underlying factors that contributed to the issue. Based on this analysis, we provide detailed recommendations for preventive measures, which may include system configuration changes, monitoring solutions, backup strategies, maintenance procedures, or staff training. We can also implement these preventive measures as part of our service to ensure your system remains stable in the future.

Can you fix issues with customizations and extensions?

Yes, we specialize in diagnosing and resolving issues with Business Central customizations and extensions. Our team has extensive experience with the Business Central extension framework and can identify conflicts, performance issues, and functionality problems in custom code. We can repair existing customizations, optimize their performance, and ensure they work correctly with the core system and other extensions. We can also provide guidance on best practices for future customization development.

Do you offer preventive maintenance services?

Yes, we offer preventive maintenance services to help you avoid system issues before they occur. These services include regular system health checks, performance monitoring, database maintenance, code reviews, and security assessments. We can establish a regular maintenance schedule tailored to your specific needs and system complexity. Our preventive approach helps identify potential issues early, allowing for proactive resolution before they impact your business operations.
