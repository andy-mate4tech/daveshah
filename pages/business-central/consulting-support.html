Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our consulting and support services

What types of support channels do you offer?

We offer multiple support channels to meet your needs, including email, phone, and a dedicated support portal. Our Premium and Enterprise support plans also include direct access to our support team through Microsoft Teams or other collaboration platforms. We work with you to determine the most effective communication channels for your team.

How quickly do you respond to support requests?

Our response times vary based on your support plan and the severity of the issue. For critical issues that impact your business operations, we prioritize response and resolution. Our Standard plan offers a 24-hour response time, Premium plan offers an 8-hour response time, and Enterprise plan offers a 4-hour response time. We also offer custom SLAs for businesses with specific requirements.

Can you help with Business Central customizations and integrations?

Yes, our consulting and support services include assistance with Business Central customizations and integrations. We can help you extend the functionality of Business Central to meet your specific business requirements, as well as integrate it with other systems in your technology stack. Our team has extensive experience with Business Central's extensibility options and API capabilities.

Do you provide training for new users?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training for new users as part of our support services. This includes both group and one-on-one training sessions, as well as custom training materials tailored to your specific processes. We also offer refresher training for existing users and advanced training for power users and administrators. Our goal is to ensure your team can effectively use Business Central to perform their daily tasks.

How do you handle Business Central updates and upgrades?

We proactively manage Business Central updates and upgrades to ensure a smooth transition to new versions. Our team stays up-to-date with Microsoft's release schedule and evaluates each update for potential impact on your system. We test updates in a sandbox environment before applying them to your production system, and we schedule upgrades during off-hours to minimize disruption to your business operations. We also provide training on new features and functionality introduced in updates.
