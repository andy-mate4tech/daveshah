Mobile WMS Features

Comprehensive warehouse management capabilities for your business

Receiving & Putaway

Streamline the receiving process with barcode scanning, validate items against purchase orders, and optimize putaway locations based on item characteristics and warehouse layout. Mobile WMS guides warehouse staff through the most efficient putaway paths.

Inventory Management

Perform inventory counts, cycle counts, and adjustments directly from mobile devices. Mobile WMS supports both scheduled and ad-hoc counting processes, with real-time updates to your Business Central or NAV system.

Picking & Packing

Optimize picking routes, support multiple picking methods (single order, batch, zone), and validate picked items with barcode scanning. Mobile WMS reduces picking errors and increases picking speed with clear, step-by-step instructions.

Shipping

Validate shipments against sales orders, generate shipping labels, and record shipping information directly from mobile devices. Mobile WMS ensures accurate and efficient shipping processes with full traceability.

Internal Movements

Manage bin-to-bin transfers, replenishment, and warehouse reorganization with guided workflows. Mobile WMS optimizes internal movements to maintain efficient warehouse organization and stock availability.

Production Support

Manage raw material picking, consumption reporting, and finished goods receipt for production orders. Mobile WMS integrates seamlessly with Business Central and NAV production processes.

Barcode Scanning

Support for various barcode types (1D, 2D, QR) and scanning devices, enabling fast and accurate data capture. Mobile WMS validates scanned information against Business Central or NAV data in real-time.

Reporting & Analytics

Track warehouse performance metrics, monitor productivity, and identify improvement opportunities. Mobile WMS provides insights into warehouse operations to support continuous optimization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Mobile WMS

What hardware is required for Mobile WMS?

Mobile WMS works with a variety of mobile devices, including handheld scanners, tablets, and smartphones with scanning capabilities. The specific hardware requirements depend on your warehouse environment and operational needs. For most warehouse environments, we recommend rugged handheld devices with integrated barcode scanners that can withstand drops, dust, and temperature variations. These devices typically run Android or Windows operating systems. For less demanding environments, consumer-grade tablets or smartphones with Bluetooth scanners may be sufficient. We can help you select the appropriate hardware based on factors such as scanning volume, environmental conditions, budget constraints, and existing infrastructure. As part of our implementation services, we provide hardware recommendations tailored to your specific requirements.

How does Mobile WMS integrate with Business Central and NAV?

Mobile WMS integrates directly with Business Central and NAV through a server component that communicates with your ERP database. The integration is seamless and real-time, ensuring that all warehouse transactions are immediately reflected in your Business Central or NAV system. The Mobile WMS server component is installed on your server infrastructure (or cloud environment) and handles communication between the mobile devices and your ERP system. This integration leverages standard Business Central and NAV APIs and respects the business logic and security settings in your ERP system. The solution works with both cloud and on-premises deployments of Business Central and NAV, and supports all recent versions of these systems. No modifications to your core ERP system are required, making upgrades and maintenance straightforward.

How long does it take to implement Mobile WMS?

The implementation timeline for Mobile WMS varies depending on the complexity of your warehouse operations, the number of processes being implemented, and the extent of process redesign required. A typical implementation takes between 2-6 weeks from initial setup to go-live. Basic implementations covering receiving, putaway, picking, and shipping can be completed in as little as 2-3 weeks. More complex implementations involving multiple warehouses, advanced workflows, or significant process redesign may take 4-6 weeks. Our implementation approach is designed to deliver value quickly, with a phased rollout that allows you to start using the system for key processes while additional functionality is being implemented. We work closely with your team to develop a realistic implementation timeline based on your specific requirements and operational constraints.

Can Mobile WMS work offline?

Yes, Mobile WMS includes offline functionality that allows warehouse operations to continue even when network connectivity is temporarily unavailable. When operating in offline mode, the mobile devices store transaction data locally and synchronize with the server once connectivity is restored. This ensures that warehouse operations can continue without interruption during network outages or in areas with poor wireless coverage. The offline capabilities include all core warehouse functions such as receiving, putaway, picking, and inventory counts. While offline, the system maintains data integrity and prevents conflicts through intelligent data management. For optimal performance, we recommend a reliable wireless network infrastructure in your warehouse, but the offline capabilities provide an important safety net for uninterrupted operations.

How customizable is Mobile WMS?

Mobile WMS offers extensive customization options to adapt to your specific warehouse processes and requirements. The system includes a configuration tool that allows for customization of workflows, screen layouts, data fields, validation rules, and user permissions without requiring programming. For more advanced customization needs, the system supports custom development through its API and extension framework. This allows for integration with other systems, implementation of specialized business logic, and development of custom functionality beyond the standard features. Our implementation team works with you to understand your specific requirements and configure Mobile WMS accordingly. We follow a best-practice approach that balances customization with maintainability, ensuring that your system can be easily upgraded and supported over time.
