Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Dime Scheduler

How does Dime Scheduler integrate with Business Central and NAV?

Dime Scheduler integrates seamlessly with Business Central and NAV through a connector that enables bidirectional data exchange between the systems. This integration allows Dime Scheduler to access resources, projects, service orders, and other relevant data from your ERP system, while sending back scheduling information such as assignments, time registrations, and status updates. The connector supports both cloud and on-premises deployments of Business Central and NAV, and works with all recent versions of these systems. The integration is configurable, allowing you to determine which data elements are synchronized and how frequently. This ensures that your scheduling system always has access to the most current information from your ERP system, while scheduling decisions and updates are reflected in your business processes. The integration respects your Business Central or NAV security settings and business logic, ensuring data integrity and appropriate access controls.

Can Dime Scheduler handle complex scheduling rules and constraints?

Yes, Dime Scheduler is designed to handle complex scheduling rules and constraints that reflect the realities of your business operations. The system supports a wide range of scheduling constraints, including resource skills and certifications, availability patterns (working hours, time off, capacity), location-based constraints (travel time, service territories), equipment requirements, and task dependencies. Dime Scheduler allows you to define business rules that govern scheduling decisions, such as prioritizing certain types of work, respecting service level agreements, or ensuring that resources with specific qualifications are assigned to particular tasks. The system can also account for factors like resource preferences, customer preferences, and cost considerations in the scheduling process. For organizations with unique scheduling requirements, Dime Scheduler offers customization options to implement specialized business rules. Our implementation team works with you to understand your specific scheduling constraints and configure Dime Scheduler accordingly, ensuring that the system supports your business processes effectively.

How does the map-based scheduling work in Dime Scheduler?

Dime Scheduler's map-based scheduling provides a geographical view of your resources and tasks, enabling location-aware scheduling decisions. The system geocodes addresses from your Business Central or NAV system, placing resources and tasks on an interactive map interface. Schedulers can visualize the current location of resources, see assigned and unassigned tasks, and understand travel distances and times between locations. The map view allows for intelligent scheduling decisions based on proximity, helping to minimize travel time and fuel costs. Schedulers can select resources based on their location relative to a task, ensuring that the nearest qualified resource is assigned. The system can also optimize routes for resources with multiple tasks, sequencing assignments to minimize total travel distance. For field service operations, the map-based scheduling capabilities are particularly valuable, as they help balance workload distribution across service territories while minimizing non-productive travel time. The map interface is fully integrated with the other scheduling views in Dime Scheduler, allowing schedulers to switch between different perspectives as needed.

How long does it take to implement Dime Scheduler?

The implementation timeline for Dime Scheduler varies depending on the complexity of your scheduling requirements, the extent of integration with Business Central or NAV, and the amount of customization needed. A typical implementation takes between 3-8 weeks from initial setup to go-live. Basic implementations with standard integration and minimal customization can be completed in as little as 3-4 weeks. More complex implementations involving extensive customization, multiple resource types, or specialized business rules may take 6-8 weeks. Our implementation approach is designed to deliver value quickly, with a phased rollout that allows you to start using the system for key scheduling processes while additional functionality is being implemented. We work closely with your team to develop a realistic implementation timeline based on your specific requirements and operational constraints. The implementation process includes installation, configuration, data migration, integration setup, user training, and go-live support. We also provide post-implementation support to ensure a smooth transition to the new scheduling system.

Can Dime Scheduler support mobile users and field staff?

Yes, Dime Scheduler provides robust support for mobile users and field staff through its mobile-friendly interface. Field personnel can access their schedules, view task details, update task status, and record time directly from smartphones and tablets. The mobile interface is responsive and optimized for smaller screens, ensuring a good user experience on mobile devices. Field staff can see their daily and weekly schedules, get directions to job locations, access customer information and task instructions, and communicate schedule changes or delays. The mobile capabilities support both online and offline modes, allowing field staff to continue working even in areas with limited connectivity. When back online, the system automatically synchronizes any updates made while offline. For organizations with specific mobile requirements, Dime Scheduler can be integrated with dedicated field service mobile apps or custom mobile solutions. This flexibility ensures that your field staff have the tools they need to be productive and responsive, regardless of their location or the devices they use.
