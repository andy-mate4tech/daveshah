Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about contacting and working with us

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, we recommend calling our office directly. Our team prioritizes responsiveness and clear communication throughout our engagement with clients.

Do you offer remote consulting services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive remote consulting services for Business Central and NAV. Our team uses secure collaboration tools to provide implementation, support, training, and development services remotely. This approach allows us to serve clients across Australia and internationally while maintaining high-quality service delivery.

What information should I prepare before contacting you?

To help us provide the most relevant information during our initial conversation, it's helpful to prepare the following: a brief description of your business and industry, your current ERP system (if any), key challenges you're facing, specific requirements or goals for your Business Central or NAV system, project timeline considerations, and budget parameters. The more information you can provide, the better we can tailor our recommendations to your specific needs.

Do you provide services outside of Australia?

Yes, we provide services to clients internationally. With our remote delivery capabilities, we can effectively support Business Central and NAV implementations, upgrades, and support services regardless of geographic location. We have experience working with clients across different time zones and can adapt our service delivery approach to accommodate your location and business hours.

What is your typical project process?

Our typical project process includes the following phases: Initial Consultation (understanding your requirements and objectives), Proposal & Planning (defining scope, timeline, and budget), Analysis & Design (detailed business process analysis and solution design), Implementation (system configuration, customization, data migration, and integration), Testing (comprehensive testing to ensure system quality), Training (user training tailored to different roles), Go-Live (controlled system launch with support), and Post-Implementation Support (ongoing assistance and optimization). We adapt this process to fit the specific needs and scale of each project.
